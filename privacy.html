Privacy policy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

Scripture Showdown is a group Bible quiz. This policy covers the iOS app, the Android app, and this page.

We do not create accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. We do not show ads. We do not sell or share personal information. Team names and scores are entered for the game in the room and stay on that device.

The app remembers which questions have already been used, in local storage on the device, so a clue does not repeat in the same session. Clearing the bank, or deleting the app, removes that memory. Nothing in that memory is sent to us.

The question bank is included in the app. A short scripture reference is shown after an answer for the group in the room. It is not transmitted.

If a future version adds phone buzzers, this policy will be updated before that version ships. Version 1.0.0 does not connect phones to a game room and does not use a server.

Children can use the app with a group. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
